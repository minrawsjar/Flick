# Demo Video: "Gone in a Flick"

> **Flick's videos: the 3-minute submission script, and the intro, ready to make with an AI video tool, with every prompt and every image to upload.**

Nina's money problems follow her everywhere as silent people in grey suits holding placards. For
each one, a Flick agent sorts it out, she flicks a finger at her phone, and the problem vanishes.
It ends on Flick's real logo animation and "Install Flick now".

## The Rules for Every Shot

- **Never let the AI draw the phone's screen.** Cut in a real screen recording instead.
- **Every vanish looks the same:** golden dust with tiny cyan, violet and pink sparks, the colours of Flick's logo, with the same whoosh and chime.
- **No music from the generator.** Add licensed music in the edit; the hackathon allows no unlicensed music.
- **Under three minutes** for the submission video, public on YouTube.
